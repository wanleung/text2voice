3–30 seconds of clean speech from one speaker, plus the exact words spoken. Phone recordings work as they are, including iPhone Voice Memos (M4A). Only use voices you have permission to clone.

    
    
    
    
    
    

    

      
      
    

  





  

    
Design a voice

    
Describe who is speaking and how. Qwen3-TTS creates a new voice from the description, so no real person's voice is cloned. Each preview is a different voice: keep generating until you like one, then save it.

    
    

      
    

    
    
Tip: lighter, conversational voices stay Cantonese on sentences with English words; deep newsreader voices sometimes drift toward Mandarin.
